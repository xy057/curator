# Link to a Time

The page's address may say where it starts: `#t=83` (seconds), `#t=1:23`, or `#bar=12`
(`#bar=12.3` for the bar's third beat). A button on the transport copies a link to where the
page is now: by its time, or, with Link by bar set in Export to Web, by its bar.

It shows a button (`curator.ui.button`), a setting of the export's (`curator.settings`), places
in the score turned into times and back (`curator.score`), and texts in the viewer's language
(`curator.tr`, `locales/`).
