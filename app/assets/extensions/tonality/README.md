# Tonality

The key the music is in, stretch by stretch, as a band of colours along the top of the score.

- `keys.lua`: what a key is, its name and its colour.
- `areas.lua`: the key areas the project keeps, and the rules they are kept by.
- `lane.lua`: the Tonality lane, its buttons and the dialog.
- `find.lua`: Find Keys, which hears the keys in the music.
- `band.lua`: the band the score shows.
