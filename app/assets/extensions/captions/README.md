# Captions

Timed text under the score: each caption shows while its music sounds, its time running out.

- `captions.lua`: the captions the project keeps, and the rules they are kept by (one shows at a time).
- `lane.lua`: the Captions lane, the dialog that asks for a caption's text and bars, and the preview's menu.
- `sheet.lua`: the Captions sheet (double-click the lane's name): every caption as a row, Import… and Export….
- `subtitles.lua`: SubRip (.srt) and WebVTT (.vtt) files read and written; a video's subtitles.
- `settings.lua`: how captions look in every project.

A caption's text may use `*italic*` and `**bold**`.
